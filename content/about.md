## From forestry and ecology to data engineering

I studied Forestry at the Federal University of Agriculture in Nigeria from 2014 to 2019. Alongside the forestry itself, the degree included data analysis, quantitative models and environmental statistics.

In April 2022 I started an MSc in Ecology at Universität Bielefeld, focused on statistical data analysis, programming in R and Python, and quantitative methods. I finished it in March 2026.

In July 2022, a few months into the master's, I started working as a Data Engineer at GI Group in Bielefeld. I built Python and SQL pipelines orchestrated with Airflow, modelled data in Snowflake and joined marketing data from GA4 and Google Tag Manager with CRM and business data.

From August 2024 to March 2026 I worked remotely for Miele, bringing SAP finance data into a Databricks Lakehouse and building the gold layer as dbt models for finance and operational reporting.

Since May 2026 I have been a Data Engineer at Phaina in Bielefeld, which builds Argus, software for digital product advice on technical products. I build the data layer behind it: more than 500,000 products and 5.9 million attribute values from 15 manufacturers, distributors and retailers in one canonical model across PostgreSQL, Qdrant and Neo4j, with RAG pipelines and AI agents on top.

## How I work

I build data quality, lineage and access control into every pipeline. Incoming data is validated against data contracts, and quality, consistency and freshness are checked automatically.

I work closely with AI engineers and business teams and turn their requirements into reliable, documented data products. With AI engineers that means aligning data formats, access patterns and requirements for inference and retrieval.

I work in an AI-native way with Claude Code every day.

## What I care about

Looking across my work, a few things keep coming back.

Data people can trust. At GI Group I implemented data validation and freshness monitoring and fixed root causes for good. At Miele I delivered dashboards with traceable KPIs.

Privacy. I tracked lineage with GDPR-compliant access control at GI Group, and in AdhereAI I applied privacy by design for health data, with consent tracking, pseudonymised patient IDs and role-based access.

AI that stays honest. The AI Lab on this site answers only from my content, cites its sources and says plainly when my profile does not cover a question.
