## Case study

- **Problem**: Marketing data from GA4 and Google Tag Manager sat in a BigQuery export on Google Cloud, separate from CRM and business data.
- **What I did**: Built Python and SQL ETL pipelines orchestrated with Apache Airflow, loaded the marketing data into Snowflake and joined it with CRM and business data, and added data validation, freshness monitoring and lineage tracking.
- **Result**: Trusted datasets and Tableau reports built with stakeholders, root causes of data issues fixed for good, and GDPR-compliant access control.
