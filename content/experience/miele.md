## Case study

- **Problem**: Finance and business stakeholders needed traceable KPIs built on SAP FI/CO data.
- **What I did**: Extracted SAP FI/CO data via OData services and BAPIs with Azure Data Factory, cleaned and standardised it through bronze and silver layers in Delta Lake with PySpark, built the gold layer as dbt models, and embedded quality checks with Great Expectations and lineage and access control with Unity Catalog.
- **Result**: Power BI semantic models and dashboards with traceable KPIs for finance and business stakeholders, with testing and deployment automated through CI/CD and Terraform.
